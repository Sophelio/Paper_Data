\section{Supplementary: task-conditioned relational discovery on Lorenz}

\subsection{Scientific object and information boundary}
$O=(D,\Omega_{obs},S,E,\Pi,A)$ with $D=\{x,y\}$ explanatory and $z$ supervised
only; $\Omega_{obs}$ a trajectory ensemble on $t\in[0,12]$, $\Delta t=0.005$;
$S$ a centred five-point stencil with the trajectory as the statistical unit;
$E$ either numerical (clean) or additive Gaussian on $x,y$ at
$\sigma\in\{0,0.005,0.01\}$ times the development channel standard deviation,
applied \emph{before} all differentiation, smoothing, quotient and phase
construction; $\Pi$ the canonical generator at $\mathrm{rtol}=\mathrm{atol}=10^{-12}$;
$A$ forbidding $z$, $\dot z$ and every $z$-derived quantity from the explanatory
dependency graph. The information boundary
$I_k=\{x_{k-2:k+2},y_{k-2:k+2}\}$ is shared by all methods; the primary raw
comparator is the full ten-value stencil. This is retrospective reconstruction,
never forecasting.

\subsection{Candidate grammar}
Seventeen declared representations: raw stencil; $+$rates; $+$rates and
curvature; $+$products; $+$masked quotients ($\dot y|x$, $y|x$, the classical
phase derivatives $D_x y$ and $D_y x$); $+$regularised (CA), reference-shifted
(RS) and sensitivity-centered (SC) pairs; the full admissible set
$\mathcal{C}_{\rm all}$ (38 coordinates); and degree-2 and degree-3 polynomial
libraries over the raw stencil. Coordinate construction $C$ is kept distinct
from the relation basis $R(C)$. RS and SC coordinates come from an
implementation verified elementwise ($\mathrm{atol}=10^{-12}$) against the
canonical Archaieus reference.

\subsection{Contracts and qualification}
$q_{\rm acc}$ minimises mean six-fold grouped cross-validated RMSE.
$q_{\rm cmp}$ and $q_{\rm rob}$ first form an accuracy-equivalent set and then
apply a lexicographic hierarchy: fewer coordinates, fewer active terms, better
conditioning, greater fold stability. $q_{\rm rob}$ scores the worst case over
the noise envelope. The equivalence threshold is
$\mathrm{best}+\max(\mathrm{SE},\tau)$ with $\tau=10^{-3}$. The plain
one-standard-error rule degenerates here ($\mathrm{SE}\approx3\times10^{-7}$,
every equivalent set a singleton); $\tau$ ties equivalence to the object's own
resolution ($\sigma_z\approx9.04$). The $q_{\rm cmp}$ selection is unchanged for
$\tau\in[10^{-4},10^{-1}]$ and $q_{\rm rob}$ is unchanged at every $\tau$.

\subsection{Estimator settings}
PySINDy 2.1.0 STLSQ, $\alpha=0$, thresholds
$\{10^{-4},10^{-3},10^{-2},5\times10^{-2},10^{-1},5\times10^{-1}\}$ chosen on
development folds. STLSQ carries no intercept, so targets are centred on the
training mean and the offset restored at prediction. Polynomial baselines use
\texttt{PolynomialLibrary} at degrees 2 and 3 without bias. The MLP is
scikit-learn \texttt{MLPRegressor}, hidden layers $(64,64)$, ReLU, Adam,
early stopping on an internal split of the training data, five fixed seeds;
input scaling fitted on development data only.

\subsection{Negative and partial findings}
(i) $q_{\rm rob}$ selected the same coordinates as $q_{\rm acc}$: observational
uncertainty changed the qualification, not the representation.
(ii) The MLP advantage does not survive $1\%$ noise ($0.803$, $0.842$, $0.839$
across representations, within ${\sim}1.3$ standard errors).
(iii) Under noise the conventional degree-2 polynomial baseline is within a
factor $1.34$ of the expanded representation.
(iv) The accuracy and robust contracts both selected an effectively
rank-deficient representation, because neither penalises conditioning.
(v) The one-standard-error rule required correction before it could express the
compactness contract at all.

\subsection{Implementation status}
The SIR engine executed the containment contract natively. It does not yet expose
discovery contracts, grouped held-out validation, declarable task utilities or
qualified-model sets, so those were executed in an audited reference harness with
PySINDy as the embedded relation solver. An independent wrapper---verified by
static and dynamic tests not to import the harness---reimplements the same
selection logic around PySINDy directly. Implementation status:
\texttt{MCP\_NATIVE\_PRIMITIVES\_PLUS\_AUDITED\_CONTRACT\_HARNESS}.
