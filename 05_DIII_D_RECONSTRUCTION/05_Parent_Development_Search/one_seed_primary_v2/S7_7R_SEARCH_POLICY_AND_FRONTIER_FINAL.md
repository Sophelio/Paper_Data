### S7.7 Frozen Search Policy And Explored Frontier

#### Multiplicity-Controlled Search

The admissible coordinate universe is numerically dominated by one diagnostic.
Electron-temperature channels are forty-seven per cent of the hardened primitive
basis but appear as an ancestor of eighty-four per cent of the admissible atomic
coordinates, because pairwise constructors turn channel multiplicity into
quadratic coordinate multiplicity, and because a strictly positive, smoothly
varying quantity satisfies the frozen denominator condition where fluctuating
diagnostics do not.

That is a property of how the device is instrumented, not a defect of
admissibility, and it was not corrected by deleting coordinates. Instead the
search policy allocates opportunity by stratum rather than by count. Every
admissible coordinate was assigned, from metadata alone, to exactly one stratum
identified by its constructor family and by the scientific families of its
operands in their declared roles — a numerator family is distinguished from a
denominator family, a level operand from a rate operand. This produced one
hundred and twenty-seven active strata.

Each constructor family then received a fixed expansion allowance filled by
round-robin across its strata, so that a family signature receives a second
candidate only after every signature has received a first. Each stratum
contributed exactly one starting path. The consequence is that the
electron-temperature share of initial search opportunity fell from eighty-four
per cent of atomic ancestry to thirty-one per cent of search seeds, below even
its share of the primitive basis.

#### Development Navigation

Candidate representations were ordered by a deliberately simple proxy: affine
ordinary least squares of the target on calibration-standardised coordinates,
fitted separately within each discharge and each block-local calibration
interval, and scored by the frozen normalised error on the protected interval.
The navigation score is the mean of that error across blocks and across the
twenty development discharges.

This is search navigation and nothing else. It is not the task's utility
functional, not validation evidence, and not a qualification criterion. It
carries no parsimony term, no conditioning term, no baseline skill, and no
scientific-family bonus; balance lives in the opportunity set, never in the
score. External discharges were not opened, and no baseline of any kind was run.

#### One-Seed Multi-Stratum Exploration

The complete policy was frozen and hashed before the first density value was
read. Its projected cost was computed from metadata alone and had to fit a
pre-declared allowance; the initial two-path-per-stratum policy did not, and was
replaced prospectively by a single path per stratum rather than by removing any
stratum from the search. The primary search is therefore broad in scientific
opportunity and shallower in multi-start depth, and that trade was accepted in
advance. A two-seed search remains possible only as a separately labelled
search-depth sensitivity analysis; it cannot replace this result.

Each of the one hundred and twenty-seven paths grew from its seed to twelve
coordinates. At every step, every stratum represented in the expansion shortlist
offered exactly one candidate — its best unused coordinate — and the extension
with the lowest navigation score was retained. The opportunity set is balanced;
the winning addition is performance-guided. A separate control lane repeated the
procedure using only the sixty-six admissible primitive levels, guaranteeing
that compact raw-only representations appear in the frontier.

#### Explored Frontier

All ten thousand seven hundred and seventy-eight admissible coordinates were
scored as single-coordinate representations. The search then evaluated a further
one hundred and fifty-two thousand distinct multivariate representations, giving
an explored frontier of one hundred and sixty-two thousand eight hundred and
forty-five, spread across all support sizes from one to twelve. No design was
rank deficient.

Within the frontier, the lowest navigation score falls from 0.48 at a single
coordinate to 0.17 at twelve. The compositions of those supports are mixed: by
eight coordinates they span seven scientific families, and magnetic, pedestal
and gas quantities appear in them far more often than their share of the
coordinate universe would suggest. Electron-temperature ancestry nonetheless
appears in every one of them. Having equalised opportunity, the search was not
re-balanced afterwards; that outcome is reported as found.

#### Search Boundary

The frontier is a vanishingly small sample of what the task permits. The
admissible universe contains on the order of 10³⁹ representations of size one to
twelve; the search evaluated 1.6 × 10⁵ of them. Everything else remains
admissible and unsearched. No property was tested there, and no negative finding
of any kind attaches to it. The search was not exhaustive and no optimum,
global or local, is claimed.

#### Gate To Utility Qualification

The explored frontier records where the frozen computational policy actually
looked. It does not convert the much larger unsearched portion of A_rec into a
negative finding. S7.8 applies the frozen scientific utility and qualification
rules to the evaluated frontier.
