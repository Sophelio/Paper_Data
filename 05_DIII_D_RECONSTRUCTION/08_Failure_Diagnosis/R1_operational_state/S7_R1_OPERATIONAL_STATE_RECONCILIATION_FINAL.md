### Operational-State Reconciliation

#### Qualification Exposes A Question About Organization

The first discovery epoch produced a frozen relational representation that
failed external qualification, and the failure was extremely concentrated: forty
of forty-two external discharges reconstructed at development level, while two
earlier-era discharges failed by a factor of seventy and dominated the pooled
mean the gate is defined on. Provenance localized both failures to a single
coordinate — the squared gas-injection valve command — under extreme
extrapolation inside one validation block.

That pattern admits a natural interpretation: perhaps the observational record
contained two physically distinct fueling states, the pooled mathematical
interpretation represented them as one ensemble, and downstream qualification
exposed the mismatch. If so, the earliest invalidated object in the discovery
chain would be the interpretation itself, and the same task could be requalified
conditional on state.

This audit tested that proposition rather than assuming it.

#### Predictor-Defined Fueling State

The test used admissible predictor-side information only — the four gas
injection valve command channels and the beam actuation quantities — with the
density target, all reconstruction errors and all qualification labels sealed
throughout state construction. The audit unit was the discharge–block pair,
because the frozen failure is localized to one block rather than to whole
discharges.

No state emerged. In the primary one-dimensional audit the block-level gas
command distribution has no isolated high-command group; its largest gap falls
inside the development set; and the two catastrophic blocks are not in the high
tail at all. The single permitted multivariate fallback — a compact actuator
vector, standardized, with one deterministic two-cluster partition and
nearest-neighbour support distances — places both catastrophic blocks among
seventy-five ordinary blocks drawn from both cohorts. One external discharge
sits further from the development set in actuator space than one of the
catastrophic blocks does, and reconstructs normally.

#### Development Support

The support-coverage comparison is decisive against the state hypothesis. The
development cohort covers the external gas-command range, and a development
discharge reaches a higher gas command than either failing discharge. The strong
gas-actuation condition was well represented during discovery.

The only block-level quantity that isolates the two failures is the ratio of the
protected-window maximum to the calibration-window maximum, which reaches forty
in both cases against a development maximum of 1.02. But that quantity measures
where the frozen validation boundary happens to fall relative to an actuator
transition, not the operational condition of the discharge. Within both failing
discharges the gas is off through the first block, turns on inside the second
block's protected window, and is fully on — at the discharge's highest level —
through the third block, which reconstructs normally because the calibration
window has by then absorbed it. What development lacked was not a plasma state
but a block alignment.

#### Reconciliation Of The SIR Chain

No instantiated object in the chain was invalidated. The observational object
already contained the actuator information; the mathematical interpretation
pooled an ensemble that genuinely is one ensemble with respect to gas actuation;
the ontology, the admissible universe, the explored frontier, the selected
representation and the qualification were each constructed correctly under the
contract as written.

The admissible universe admitted the squared command by rule rather than by
oversight: the frozen admissibility condition guards denominators, and the
contract states explicitly that identity, derivative, product and level–rate
constructors carry no denominator and therefore no gate. The gap is in the
contract, not in any object built from it. Mathematical domain support is not
observational range support: a squared quantity is defined everywhere, yet a
relation calibrated on one interval becomes numerically unsupported when its
argument leaves that interval.

#### Decision

The operational-state route stops here. State-conditioned requalification is not
authorised, because there is no defensible state to condition on, and the one
separating quantity is an artifact of the validation geometry rather than a
property of the plasma.

The reconciliation target is instead a minimal contract revision: an
observational range-support admissibility condition for total nonlinear
constructors, alongside the existing denominator guard. That belongs to a future
epoch, declared prospectively before any new discovery, and is not retrofitted
here. The first epoch's negative result stands exactly as frozen.
