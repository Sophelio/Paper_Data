## Main text — candidate paragraph (≈ 150 words)

> In the reconstruction task, an initial discovery epoch selected a relational
> representation that satisfied development selection but failed frozen external
> qualification, with two discharges reconstructing more than an order of
> magnitude worse than the calibration-mean baseline. Provenance traced the
> failure to a coordinate evaluated far outside the range over which its relation
> had been calibrated. A plausible operational-state explanation was tested
> target-blindly and refuted, localizing the defect to the admissibility
> component of the contract rather than to the relational ontology. A single
> dimensionless range-support condition was added and frozen before any new
> search. Rediscovery, cross-fitted so that no discharge's own target values
> informed the support used to reconstruct it, cleared the prospectively fixed
> skill threshold (Δ₀ = −0.76 against the calibration mean, Δ₁ = −0.027 against
> persistence) and the catastrophic tail disappeared, the worst normalized error
> falling from 11.95 to 0.92. Six folds produced six different supports: stable
> utility, non-unique representations.

**What this deliberately omits:** gate names, stage numbers, bootstrap
frequencies, the era split, the τ definition, the audit tree. Those belong in the
supplement. The main text should communicate the SIR **mechanism** —
discovery → qualification exposed an extrapolation failure → contract refined →
cross-fitted rediscovery → qualified positive reconstruction — not the audit
mechanics.

## Supplement / main-result handoff (≈ 400 words)

> The reconstruction task asked whether relational coordinates built from
> same-shot plasma diagnostics support block-local prequential reconstruction of
> line-averaged density across a frozen 62-discharge DIII-D object. A first
> discovery epoch, selecting on twenty development discharges under a
> lexicographic utility fixed in advance, produced a twelve-coordinate
> representation that failed the frozen external qualification outright: the
> mean calibration-normalized error was 0.74 against 0.21 for persistence, driven
> by two discharges at 11.95 and 11.77. Provenance analysis localized the failure
> to a single coordinate, a gas-actuator self-product, evaluated some four orders
> of magnitude of calibration standard deviations outside its calibrated range.
> The natural explanation — that the development cohort had pooled distinct
> operational states — was tested using target-blind predictor evidence alone and
> refuted: the development cohort already covered, and one development discharge
> exceeded, the actuator range of both failing discharges. The defect therefore
> lay not in the ontology but in the contract's admissibility component, which
> guarded denominators against division pathology while permitting unbounded
> extrapolation of any coordinate. A single dimensionless, constructor-generic
> observational range-support condition was added, admitting a coordinate only
> where application stays within one calibration-range width of its calibration
> hull, and was frozen, with its threshold and its qualification gate, before any
> new search.
>
> Discovery then resumed over the 3 451 coordinates that condition admits, in six
> discharge-grouped folds, each support hashed before any held-out target value
> was opened. The result clears the prospectively fixed threshold: paired mean
> differences of −0.764 against the calibration-mean baseline and −0.027 against
> persistence, both below the −0.01 fixed before any result existed. All 2 232
> held-out range-support checks pass, no held-out discharge exceeds a normalized
> error of one, and the worst case falls from 11.95 to 0.92.
>
> Two qualifications travel with the result. The margin over persistence is
> modest and era-asymmetric, carried by the later processing era while the earlier
> era is practically tied; against raw-coordinate comparators over the same
> admissible information the margin is far wider (−0.096 versus ridge regression,
> −0.157 versus gradient boosting), and that is the defensible representational
> claim. And predictor-side applicability was instantiated from the whole finite
> object, so what has been tested is whether the target relationship transfers to
> discharges whose targets were withheld — not external validation, and not a
> claim about unseen discharges. Six folds produced six different supports,
> sharing on average about a quarter of their coordinates and none identical: the
> object admits a family of adequate relational representations rather than
> identifying one.

## Phrases to use, and to avoid

| use | avoid |
|---|---|
| clears the prospectively frozen nontrivial skill threshold | SIR dramatically beats persistence |
| the pooled margin over persistence is modest | both eras improve materially |
| adds substantial utility over the **tested** raw-coordinate representations | universally better than raw features |
| the revised procedure **eliminated** the catastrophic tail | K2 **proved** the rule **caused** the improvement |
| target-cross-fitted reconstruction over a predictor-qualified finite object | external validation · held-out cohort · unseen discharges |
| a representative full-object descriptive relation | the DIII-D density equation |

Do not let the two catastrophic discharges dominate the final text. **They are
the trigger, not the result.**
