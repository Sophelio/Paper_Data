## What the supplement should carry

The reconstruction example's value to the paper is the **iterative qualified
discovery arc**, and the supplement is where that arc can be shown in full. A
workable structure:

| § | content | source |
|---|---|---|
| S.1 | task, object, contract `K_REC_V1`, information boundary, validation geometry | S7.1–S7.3 |
| S.2 | relational ontology and the admissible coordinate universe (10 778 atoms) | S7.5, S7.5H, S7.6 |
| S.3 | search policy, utility `U_rec`, the prospectively frozen gates | S7.7, S7.8 |
| S.4 | **Epoch 1**: development selection, the pre-external freeze, and the external failure | S7.9, S7.10 |
| S.5 | **diagnosis**: extrapolation provenance; the support-family sensitivity | S7.11 |
| S.6 | **refutation**: the operational-state hypothesis tested target-blindly and rejected | S7.R1 |
| S.7 | **contract revision**: `P-RANGE-SUPPORT`, τ = 1, `V-RANGE`, `K_REC_V2` | S7.K2 |
| S.8 | **protocol**: six-fold cross-fitting; the transition-specific information boundary | S7.E2.0, S7.E2.0A |
| S.9 | **Epoch 2**: the cross-fitted result, the full gate table, the era split | S7.E2.1 |
| S.10 | support non-uniqueness across seven independent searches | S7.E2.1, S7.E2.2 |
| S.11 | `C_E2_ALL_DESC` coordinate table, as a representative descriptive relation | S7.E2.2 |
| S.12 | claim boundary and coefficient qualifications | S7.12 |

Three things earn their space in the supplement that cannot fit the main text:

1. **The refutation (S.6).** A hypothesis that would have made the failure
   comfortable was stated, tested against target-blind evidence, and rejected.
   That is the most methodologically load-bearing episode in the branch, and it
   is what distinguishes contract repair from post-hoc rescue.
2. **The prospective freeze discipline (S.4, S.8).** Thresholds, baselines and
   the reporting tier were fixed before any outcome was visible, and the record
   shows they were not moved when the result landed just short of the tier.
3. **Support non-uniqueness (S.10).** Seven independent searches, seven distinct
   supports. This is a positive scientific finding about the object, not a
   weakness to be minimized.

**Include the negative result in full.** Epoch 1's failure is preserved
unmodified, and the supplement should present it as such rather than as a
preamble to the eventual pass.

The figure was **not created**; this is a handoff. Machine-readable form:

| panel | content | key numbers |

| **A** | the `q_desc` result, unchanged by this branch | — |
| **B** | the `q_rec` iterative arc as a compact schematic: discovery → qualification failure → contract refinement → cross-fitted rediscovery → qualified positive result | — |

Panel **B** is the point of the figure — it is the SIR mechanism made visible.
Panel **C** needs a log or broken axis; the tail *is* the story. Panel **D**
shows the wide raw-coordinate margin and the modest persistence margin side by
side, which is the honest presentation of both at once.

**Optional inset:** the range-support concept — calibration hull, the τ = 1 band,
an excursion outside it. One small schematic, no data required.

**Coordinate labels**, if the figure annotates relational coordinates, should
come from `C_E2_ALL_DESC` and must be labelled a *representative full-object
descriptive relation* — never a validated DIII-D equation, a canonical relation,
or a universal relation.

**Do not put in Figure 6:** the full stage tree, every gate, the audit mechanics,
bootstrap frequency tables.
